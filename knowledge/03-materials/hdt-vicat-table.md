# HDT, Vicat, nozzle/bed and density reference (Bambu filaments)
Applies to: both · Bambu Studio 2.8
Verified: 2026-10-07 (PETG Matte row; other rows 2026-10-03, density rows 2026-10-04)

**Condition for every HDT/Vicat number below:** these are Bambu lab values from standard specimens at 100 % infill and, except PETG Basic and PETG Matte, annealed (and dried) before testing as stated per line. They are not the behaviour of an as-printed, unannealed part at normal infill. Always quote the load (1.8 MPa or 0.45 MPa) and the anneal condition with a number.

- PLA Matte: HDT 52 °C @1.8 MPa / 58 °C @0.45 MPa; Vicat 63 °C; nozzle 190-230 °C; bed 35-45 °C; TDS V3.0; annealed 55 °C 8 h - [PLA Matte TDS V3.0](https://store.bblcdn.com/s7/default/5b061f2feeac4ba88f355a33248bbda7/Bambu_PLA_Matte_Technical_Data_Sheet.pdf)
- PLA Silk+: HDT 56 °C @1.8 MPa / 60 °C @0.45 MPa; Vicat 66 °C; nozzle 210-240 °C; bed 35-45 °C; TDS V1.0; printed 230 °C, 200 mm/s, 100 % infill, annealed 55 °C 8 h - [PLA Silk+ TDS V1.0](https://store.bblcdn.com/s7/default/3d4344cb7ecc4daf98a0d53c2fdb39b4/Bambu_PLA_Silk__Technical_Data_Sheet.pdf)

Not confirmed by a corrected Bambu document.
- PA6-CF: HDT 164 °C @1.8 MPa / 186 °C @0.45 MPa; Vicat 212 °C; nozzle 260-290 °C; bed 80-100 °C; TDS V3.0; printed 280 °C, 100 mm/s, 100 % infill, annealed 80 °C 12 h; the sheet does not say whether the specimens were dry or moisture-conditioned - [PA6-CF TDS V3.0](https://wiki.bambulab.com/filament-acc/petcf-ppacf/c750bddfb8e44af6ae9f7dd9625fa458.pdf)
- TPU 95A HF: HDT and Vicat N/A (too soft for the ISO 75/306 fixtures); nozzle 220-240 °C; bed 30-35 °C; TDS V1.0 - [TPU 95A HF TDS V1.0](https://store.bblcdn.com/58df32731eab4c90a7dac9b12e13ba88.pdf)
- None of these TDS PDFs prints a calendar date; only the file's HTTP Last-Modified header dates it - [example: PLA Basic TDS](https://wiki.bambulab.com/filament-acc/abs-asa-pc/bambu_pla_basic_technical_data_sheet.pdf)
- Bambu's wiki material table carries no HDT or Vicat values - [Material table](https://wiki.bambulab.com/en/general/filament-guide-material-table)
- Wiki nozzle bands (the page states a ±10 °C tolerance): PLA family 190-240 °C, PLA-CF/GF 210-240 °C, PETG 240-270 °C, PETG HF 230-260 °C, PETG-CF 240-270 °C, PETG Translucent 230-260 °C, ABS 240-280 °C, ASA 240-280 °C, PC 260-290 °C, PA-CF/GF family 260-300 °C. The PETG Translucent TDS top end (270 °C) is 10 °C higher than the wiki band - [Material table](https://wiki.bambulab.com/en/general/filament-guide-material-table)
- Wiki bed bands by plate (Cool Plate / Engineering Plate / Smooth PEI-HT / Textured PEI): PLA 35-45 / 45-65 (glue on Engineering) / 45-65 / 45-65 °C; PETG family 60-80 °C on Engineering and PEI plates, not recommended on Cool Plate; ABS/ASA 90-100 °C, not recommended on Cool Plate; PC 100-120 °C; PA-CF/GF family 100-120 °C. So on Textured PEI, PLA's bed is 45-65 °C, not the plate-agnostic TDS 35-45 °C - [Material table](https://wiki.bambulab.com/en/general/filament-guide-material-table)

### Density
- PETG Basic density 1.25 g/cm³ (ISO 1183) - [PETG Basic TDS V3.0](https://store.bblcdn.com/s1/default/cb94589bf7994fdcbfa833badefae9cd/Bambu_PETG_Basic_Technical_Data_Sheet.pdf)
- PETG Translucent density 1.25 g/cm³ (ISO 1183) - [PETG Translucent TDS V1.0](https://store.bblcdn.com/s1/default/338c9cd853fd44d9b6ed354a49048e72/Bambu_PETG_Translucent_Technical_Data_Sheet.pdf), linked from the [store page](https://us.store.bambulab.com/products/petg-translucent)
- The Bambu Studio system presets for PETG Basic and PETG Translucent set `filament_density` = 1.25 (resolved through `inherits` to each `@base` profile), overriding 1.27 in the generic `fdm_filament_pet` parent - [BambuStudio BBL filament profiles](https://github.com/bambulab/BambuStudio/tree/master/resources/profiles/BBL/filament) (checked on Bambu Studio 02.08.02.61; verify on your installed version)

## In practice
- Quote HDT with its load and anneal condition; in the Facts rows above the 0.45 MPa value is 1-8 °C higher than the 1.8 MPa value (smallest: PLA-CF 1 °C, PETG Matte 1.1 °C; largest: ASA 8 °C), except PA6-CF at 22 °C and PC, whose printed pair is inverted (5 °C apart; TPU has no values). An an annealed lab value is not what a lower-infill, unannealed part survives.
- For a part holding its shape under light load near service temperature, design to the 1.8 MPa figure; for PC use about 112 °C until Bambu corrects the sheet.
- On Textured PEI, follow the wiki's plate-specific bed column, not the generic TDS bed range.
- Dry PA6-CF and other hygroscopic filaments before printing regardless of this table.
- For weight or cost estimates on PETG Basic or Translucent, use 1.25 g/cm³; the system presets already use it, so slicer-reported grams match.
- Per-line PLA values: [bambu-pla-family.md](bambu-pla-family.md). ABS/ASA/PC lines: [bambu-abs-asa-pc-families.md](bambu-abs-asa-pc-families.md). PA, TPU, CF/GF and supports: [bambu-pa-tpu-cf-gf-families.md](bambu-pa-tpu-cf-gf-families.md).

## Not verified / open questions
- Which PC HDT pair is correct; Bambu's V3.0 and V2.0 sheets both print the inverted pair.
- Whether PA6-CF thermal specimens were dry or conditioned.
- PETG HF's lab anneal condition conflicts with its own "do not anneal" use instruction.
- Density for the PLA family, PETG HF, PETG-CF, PA6-CF and TPU is covered in the family notes, not here.
