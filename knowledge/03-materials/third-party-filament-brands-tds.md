# Third-party filament brands: TDS values with conditions, clarity numbers, Bambu Studio presets, AMS 2 Pro fit
Applies to: both (H2C and P1S) · Bambu Studio 02.08.02.61 (system profile bundle BBL 02.08.00.05)
Verified: 2026-10-16

Related: [translucent-vs-transparent.md](translucent-vs-transparent.md), [hdt-vicat-table.md](hdt-vicat-table.md), [drying.md](drying.md), [petg-third-party-on-h2c.md](petg-third-party-on-h2c.md).

**Read every number with its condition.** TDS values are the makers' typical values, not specifications. Printed-specimen values (Polymaker, Prusament, Overture, eSUN PETG/PLA+/ABS+ V2.0, 3DXTECH) depend on the print settings stated with them. eSUN eTPU-95A and ePA-CF come from injection-moulded bars and are not comparable with printed parts. Charpy (ISO 179) and Izod (ISO 180 / ASTM D256 / GB/T 1843) impact values are different tests; never compare them directly. Full tables are in each linked TDS; this note keeps the values most used for choosing a material.

## Facts

### Bambu Studio presets for third-party lines
- No third-party filament preset in the system bundle names the H2C. Generic presets do: Generic PETG, PETG HF, PCTG, PC, ASA, ABS, TPU, TPU for AMS, PA, PA-CF and PETG-CF each have "@BBL H2C" files, so a third-party line on the H2C starts from the matching `Generic ... @BBL H2C` preset. — [BBL filament folder, v02.08.02.61](https://github.com/bambulab/BambuStudio/tree/v02.08.02.61/resources/profiles/BBL/filament)
- Third-party lines with system presets (for other Bambu printers): Polymaker PolyLite ABS, ASA, PETG, PLA and PolyTerra PLA; Polymaker Fiberon PA12-CF, PA6-CF, PA6-GF, PA612-CF, PET-CF, PETG-ESD, PETG-rCF (H2D and X1C files); Overture PLA and Matte PLA; eSUN PLA+; SUNLU PETG, PLA+, PLA+ 2.0, PLA Matte, Silk PLA+, Marble PLA, Wood PLA. P1P-specific files live in the `P1P` subfolder. — [BBL filament folder](https://github.com/bambulab/BambuStudio/tree/v02.08.02.61/resources/profiles/BBL/filament), [Polymaker subfolder](https://github.com/bambulab/BambuStudio/tree/v02.08.02.61/resources/profiles/BBL/filament/Polymaker), [SUNLU subfolder](https://github.com/bambulab/BambuStudio/tree/v02.08.02.61/resources/profiles/BBL/filament/SUNLU), [P1P subfolder](https://github.com/bambulab/BambuStudio/tree/v02.08.02.61/resources/profiles/BBL/filament/P1P)
- No system preset exists for Prusament, Fiberlogy, Formfutura, 3DXTECH, Extrudr, Taulman, Polymaker PC / PolyMax / PolySonic / TPU, Overture PETG / PC, or eSUN PETG / ASA / ABS+ / PC / TPU. — [BBL filament folder](https://github.com/bambulab/BambuStudio/tree/v02.08.02.61/resources/profiles/BBL/filament)
- There is no "@BBL P1S" third-party file: a P1S is reached through "@BBL X1C" files whose compatible printers list it, and nozzle coverage varies (for example `PolyLite PETG @BBL X1C` covers P1S 0.4/0.6/0.8 mm with 0.2 mm in its own file; `SUNLU PETG @BBL X1C` covers P1S 0.4 mm only). — [PolyLite PETG @BBL X1C.json](https://github.com/bambulab/BambuStudio/blob/v02.08.02.61/resources/profiles/BBL/filament/PolyLite%20PETG%20%40BBL%20X1C.json), [SUNLU PETG @BBL X1C.json](https://github.com/bambulab/BambuStudio/blob/v02.08.02.61/resources/profiles/BBL/filament/SUNLU/SUNLU%20PETG%20%40BBL%20X1C.json)
- Extrudr publishes its own Bambu Studio profile zips for H2C and P1S (PCTG, DuraPro ASA, DuraPro PC FR V0, DuraPro PC+PBT, PETG). Several values differ from Extrudr's own TDS (for example PCTG plate 85 °C vs TDS 90-110 °C; PCTG density 1.21 vs TDS 1.23). — [Extrudr H2C profile zip](https://s3.extrudr.com/cms-media/media-pool/Bambu_Lab_H2_C_1790862443746_45656da1bf.zip), [Extrudr P1S profile zip](https://s3.extrudr.com/cms-media/media-pool/Bambulab_P1_S_1790862775202_5b1c9368bf.zip)

### AMS 2 Pro and third-party spools
- Supported materials include PLA, PETG, ABS, ASA, PET, PA, PC, dried PVA/BVOH, PP, POM, HIPS, Bambu PLA-CF/PAHT-CF/PETG-CF, Support for PLA/PETG and TPU for AMS. Not supported: TPE, generic TPU, damp PVA/BVOH, Bambu PET-CF and TPU 95A, and other carbon- or glass-fibre filaments. So third-party CF lines (Fiberon PA6-CF20, Prusament PA11-CF, eSUN ePA-CF, CarbonX PETG+CF) are outside the supported list. — [AMS 2 Pro store page](https://us.store.bambulab.com/products/ams-2-pro)
- Spool size accepted: width 50-68 mm, diameter 197-202 mm. Drying maximum 65 °C; Bambu says it cannot fully dry the materials that need more (ABS, ASA, PA, PC and others). — [AMS 2 Pro store page](https://us.store.bambulab.com/products/ams-2-pro)
- RFID enables remaining-filament estimates, auto backup and auto-matched drying for Bambu filament; no AMS 2 Pro page forbids non-RFID spools. Third-party filament needs manual configuration, and a "?" slot can still be selected for printing (X1 manual page). — [AMS 2 Pro intro](https://wiki.bambulab.com/en/ams-2-pro/manual/intro-and-connection-guide), [AMS 2 Pro setup](https://wiki.bambulab.com/en/ams-2-pro/manual/setup-and-printting), [X1 multi-color printing](https://wiki.bambulab.com/en/x1/manual/multi-color-printing)
- Not recommended for AMS / AMS lite: filaments that are too soft (TPU 95A and softer, damp PVA/BVOH), too brittle (some PET-CF, PLA Wood, PA-CF/GF) or too hard and rough (some CF/GF lines), which wear the feed channel. — [Bambu material table](https://wiki.bambulab.com/en/general/filament-guide-material-table)
- Cardboard spools (Polymaker): current spools have rigid cardboard with rubber-coated edges; slipping is most likely near empty (under about 100 g) or when hot; adapters are for worn rollers or damaged spools, with printable adapter designs and suggested materials per drying temperature. — [Polymaker wiki: cardboard spool AMS issues](https://wiki.polymaker.com/printing-tips/common-printing-issues/cardboard-spool-ams-issues.md)
- Fiberlogy's PCTG page claims Bambu AMS compatibility (vendor claim, not a Bambu statement). — [Fiberlogy PCTG](https://fiberlogy.com/en/product/pctg-filament/)

## In practice
- **H2C:** no third-party system preset exists. Start from the matching `Generic ... @BBL H2C` preset, set nozzle, bed, fan, density and volumetric limits from the brand's TDS, and save it as a user preset. Verify on your own printer.
- **P1S:** third-party presets reach it only through "@BBL X1C" files; check the preset's compatible printers and nozzle sizes before relying on it.
- **AMS 2 Pro:** non-RFID spools are allowed with manual setup. Keep CF/GF lines and non-"for AMS" TPU out of it; feed them from an external spool holder.
- **Drying above 65 °C** cannot be done in the AMS 2 Pro: for example PolyLite ASA 70 °C, PolyLite PC / PolyMax PC 75 °C, Fiberon PA6-CF20 100 °C, Fiberlogy ASA and 3DXMAX ASA 80 °C, 3DXMAX PC 120 °C. Use a dedicated dryer or oven.
- **Comparing toughness:** compare like with like. Fiberlogy's 92 kJ/m² PCTG Izod is not comparable with a Charpy value from another brand.
- **Clear parts:** the only standing optical numbers are HDglass (ASTM D1003) and Polymaker (GB/T 2410), none with a thickness or printed-part basis. A printed, lit test coupon decides.
- **Chamber:** Polymaker PC lines ask for a 70-100 °C chamber. Check what your printer's chamber can actually reach before choosing them.
