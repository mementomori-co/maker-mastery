### Conductor current, PCB traces, ribbon
- Lapp's T12 table (based on DIN VDE 0298-4; values for 0.08 to 0.34 mm2 come from VDE 0891-1) gives 0.14 mm2 at 3 A (column C), multi-core factors (10 cores 0.55, 14 cores 0.50, 24 cores 0.40) and a 40 °C ambient factor of 0.89 for an 80 °C conductor. Worked example: a 15-core 24 AWG cable at 40 °C, conservatively with the 24-core factor: 3 x 0.40 x 0.89 = 1.07 A per core. - [Lapp T12 table](https://t3.lappcdn.com/fileadmin/catalog/en_T_pdf/T12_Current_ratings__basic_table_int.pdf)
- IPC-2221 external-layer formula as given by a fab calculator: Area[mil2] = (I / (k x dT^b))^(1/c), width = area / (oz x 1.378), k 0.048, b 0.44, c 0.725. For 1 oz copper and a 10 K rise: 0.4 A 0.085 mm, 2 A 0.781 mm, 4 A 2.033 mm, 6 A 3.556 mm, 12 A 9.251 mm. - [Advanced PCB trace-width calculator](https://www.advancedpcb.com/en-us/tools/trace-width-calculator/)
- 3M 3365 ribbon: 28 AWG 7 x 36 tinned stranded, 300 V, -20 to +105 °C, UL style 2651 (file E42769), 1.27 mm pitch, 0.89 mm thick; 26-way is 33.02 mm wide. No current rating and no bend radius are given. - [3M 3365/26 datasheet, distributor host](https://datasheet.octopart.com/3365-26-3M-datasheet-24968712.pdf)
- igus CF130.UL: minimum e-chain bend radius 7.5 x d, UL/CSA AWM and NFPA 79 approvals; no OD table on the page. - [igus CF130.UL](https://www.igus.com/product/CF130_UL)

### Strain relief
- OSHA's workplace rule requires flexible cords to be connected so strain relief keeps pull off joints and terminal screws. It is a workplace rule, not a hobby code, but a sound practice. - [OSHA 1910.305](https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.305)

## In practice
- Pick a gland by its clamping range against the cable's nominal and maximum OD, not by thread size. Example: an 8.71 to 9.04 mm cable needs an M16 Lapp MS-M (4.5 to 10 mm) or a Heyco LTCG M20; none of the M12-class parts and no Heyco M16 covers it.
- A moulded C13 plug's 31.98 mm diagonal clears a QVT 32 cut-out by only 0.3 mm and a KEL-QTA 32 B by 0.02 mm: treat either as a test fit, not a design clearance. A cord-clamping grommet such as UT 7-9 clamps the 7.8 mm cord itself instead, which means fitting the connector inside the enclosure.
- In a printed wall, leave generous material between a gland hole and any edge; a 5 mm minimum edge distance is a reasonable starting design choice (not from a standard) to verify on a test print.
- Bundle a rolled ribbon by area: a 26-way 3365 is 29.39 mm2, an equal-area circle of 6.12 mm before packing loss; roll a real one and measure.
- Add strain relief at every cable entry so no pull reaches solder joints or terminals.

## Not verified / open questions
- Whether a specific Heyco part number carries the Listed or the Recognized mark, and its UL 514B cable range.
- 3M gives no bend radius for the 3365; any 10 x thickness rule is an assumption. Alpha 5076C's own bend rule was not quoted.
- PowerStream's resistance values (community) are for solid wire; stranded values differ.
- Other gland makers (Sealcon and others) were not surveyed for a listed split gland that passes a C13 plug.
