# 04 Design for FDM - index

How to shape, orient and split a part so it prints well on a Bambu printer. Every fact links a public source (Bambu wiki, bambulab/BambuStudio source, or a manufacturer data sheet).

- [orientation-and-plate-finish.md](orientation-and-plate-finish.md) - choosing orientation by measured overhang, plate contact, islands and span; which faces go on the plate; plate finish, adhesion, removal and elephant foot.
- [overhangs-bridges-supports.md](overhangs-bridges-supports.md) - threshold angle, support types and styles, bridge length, flow and speed, overhang slowdown, overhang and bridging failures, support filament pairing and H2C preset values.
- [splitting-and-joining.md](splitting-and-joining.md) - splitting only at natural boundaries, the Cut tool (Planar, Dovetail, four connector types and their tolerance), Split to Objects/Parts, and adhesive data-sheet values with their test conditions.
