# H2C architecture
Applies to: H2C · firmware 01.02.00.00 (2026-06-01) · Bambu Studio 02.08.02.61
Verified: 2026-10-16 (printable-area facts re-checked 2026-10-16; the rest verified 2026-10-03)

## Facts
- **Positions: 8. Hotends: 7 max.** Positions = 6 Vortek rack slots (numbered 1-6) + the LEFT toolhead position + the RIGHT toolhead position (shown as "R" in the hotend map). Hotends = 1 fixed LEFT hotend (standard type, "lifting") + up to 6 swappable induction hotends for the RIGHT side. One rack slot is therefore always empty: it is the parking space for the hotend that leaves the toolhead on the next change. While printing, 1 induction hotend is on the right toolhead and 5 are parked. A returning hotend parks in the lowest-numbered empty slot, so slot numbers are not stable across a print. "8 positions" is arithmetic (6 slots + R + left), not an official phrase. - [H2C manual V1.0 2026.01, section 6.1.2](https://csm.bblcdn.com/hub/eff78da43720461787dc8bbe5fa0372d.pdf), [rack workflow](https://wiki.bambulab.com/en/h2c/manual/induction-hotend-rack-workflow), [Bambu Lab blog](https://blog.bambulab.com/bambu-lab-h2c-where-multi-material-vortek-system-meets-engineering-precision/) ("up to seven colors or materials in a single job"), [Vortek FAQ](https://wiki.bambulab.com/en/h2/manual/vortek-faq); community: [additive-x blog](https://www.additive-x.com/blog/bambu-h2c-vortek-explained)
- AMS Combo hotend contents: right 4x 0.4 mm hardened-steel induction (1 installed, 3 in the box) + 1x 0.2 mm induction + 1x 0.6 mm induction; left 2x 0.4 mm hardened-steel standard (1 installed, 1 in the box). The combo page does not state the material of the 0.2 and 0.6; the separate SKU page lists 0.2 mm as stainless steel and 0.6 mm as hardened steel. - [H2C store](https://us.store.bambulab.com/products/h2c), [H2C FAQ](https://wiki.bambulab.com/en/h2c/manual/h2c-faq), [in the box](https://wiki.bambulab.com/en/h2c/acc-in-the-box), [induction hotend SKU](https://us.store.bambulab.com/products/h2c-induction-hotend-standard-high-flow)
- Mixed nozzle diameters in one print are not supported; mixed flow types at the same diameter are. - [H2C store](https://us.store.bambulab.com/products/h2c), [Bambu Studio H2C operation](https://wiki.bambulab.com/en/h2c/manual/bambu-studio-h2c-operation)
- Upper chamber filament inlet -> right extruder; lower inlet -> left extruder. One AMS 2 Pro feeds exactly one extruder. - [H2C manual V1.0 p.15, p.32 TIPS](https://csm.bblcdn.com/hub/eff78da43720461787dc8bbe5fa0372d.pdf), [AMS 2 Pro connection guide](https://wiki.bambulab.com/en/ams-2-pro/manual/intro-and-connection-guide)
- **Printable area per nozzle.** Right nozzle alone: X 25-330, Y 0-320 = 305 x 320 mm, Z 325 mm (manual p.90-91; wiki; store "305*320*325" Right). Left nozzle alone: X 0-325, Y 0-320, Z 320 mm (store "325*320*320" Left). Both nozzles printing: shared X 25-325 = 300 mm wide (store dual-nozzle 300*320*325). A model placed outside one nozzle's area is printed entirely by the nozzle that covers it (manual p.91 section 6.3.3). X 0-25 is left-only. A model taller than 325 mm raises a "Model height exceeds" message (manual p.92). Bed 330 x 320 mm. - [H2C manual V1.0](https://csm.bblcdn.com/hub/eff78da43720461787dc8bbe5fa0372d.pdf), [max printable area, H2C section](https://wiki.bambulab.com/en/h2/manual/max-printable-area), [H2C store spec table](https://us.store.bambulab.com/products/h2c)
- The H2C 0.4 machine profile carries the same polygons: `extruder_printable_area` left 0-325 x 0-320, right 25-330 x 0-320; `printable_area` 330 x 320; heights inherited from `fdm_bbl_3dp_002_common.json`: `extruder_printable_height` 320 (left) / 325 (right), `printable_height` 325. `extruder_clearance_height_to_lid` (201) is a by-object collision limit only. The `PrintConfig.cpp` tooltip says the extruder printable height is limited by the printer mechanism. - [Bambu Lab H2C 0.4 nozzle.json](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bambulab/BambuStudio/v02.08.02.61/resources/profiles/BBL/machine/Bambu%20Lab%20H2C%200.4%20nozzle.json), [fdm_bbl_3dp_002_common.json](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bambulab/BambuStudio/v02.08.02.61/resources/profiles/BBL/machine/fdm_bbl_3dp_002_common.json), [PrintConfig.cpp](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bambulab/BambuStudio/v02.08.02.61/src/libslic3r/PrintConfig.cpp)
- **Official height conflicts.** (1) The wiki "Printer" line gives max height 320 mm, but the manual (p.91, maximum overall print height 325 mm), the store (330*320*325) and the profile (325) say 325. (2) Shared-area height: wiki 320 mm, store 325 mm; manual p.91 follows the wiki logic (above 320 mm only the right hotend prints), so a true two-nozzle print tops out at 320 mm. - [max printable area](https://wiki.bambulab.com/en/h2/manual/max-printable-area), [H2C manual V1.0](https://csm.bblcdn.com/hub/eff78da43720461787dc8bbe5fa0372d.pdf), [H2C store](https://us.store.bambulab.com/products/h2c)
- The H2C supports up to 4 AMS 2 Pro and 8 AMS HT at once (24 slots); with an external spool on the other hotend, 25 filaments. - [how to connect AMS](https://wiki.bambulab.com/en/h2c/how-to-connect-ams), [H2C FAQ](https://wiki.bambulab.com/en/h2c/manual/h2c-faq)
- Without a Filament Track Switch, each AMS is bound to the hotend it is plugged into; with one AMS the other hotend can only use an external spool. Filament Track Switch: needs H2C firmware 01.01.50.00 beta or later (first public release 01.02.00.00), Bambu Studio 2.5.3.60+, Bambu Handy 3.18.0+; cannot be used with an external spool; high-precision nozzle offset calibration is unavailable while installed; it changes filament compatibility (CF and TPU restrictions). - [Filament Track Switch](https://wiki.bambulab.com/en/general/manual/filament-track-switch-h2), [firmware history](https://wiki.bambulab.com/en/h2c/manual/h2c-firmware-release-history), [compatibility with switch](https://wiki.bambulab.com/en/h2/manual/filament-compatibility)
- **Vortek hotend memory.** Each induction hotend's memory can store filament type, material, diameter and colour; new hotends also show a factory-test colour from QC that the user cannot currently clear. "Read" (per hotend, added in firmware 01.01.00.00) or "Read All" (first setup / batch) fetches this from the toolhead; the manual says storage happens but not *when* the hotend writes it. "Hotends Info" shows specs, serial numbers and version numbers of every induction hotend previously read; no usage/wear counter was found. Manual p.31 warns to keep hands out while the toolhead reads hotends one by one. - [H2C manual V1.0 sections 7.1.3-7.1.4 p.105-107, section 2.3 p.31](https://csm.bblcdn.com/hub/eff78da43720461787dc8bbe5fa0372d.pdf), [H2C store](https://us.store.bambulab.com/products/h2c)
- **Right vs left nozzle info.** The right induction hotend's nozzle info is auto-read only and cannot be edited manually; the left (fixed, standard) hotend's info can be read with "Read Left Nozzle Info" or set manually via a pencil-icon edit. - [H2C manual V1.0 section 7.1.3 p.105](https://csm.bblcdn.com/hub/eff78da43720461787dc8bbe5fa0372d.pdf)
- **Hotend swap re-read is a maintenance step, not a mid-job feature.** After replacing a hotend with one of a different diameter or material (section 11.12.2), select its rack number on the printer screen and tap "Read"; the wiki repeats this. No official source (manual, three wiki pages, store page, H2C FAQ) says anything about swapping a hotend mid-print or what happens to an in-progress job's mapping. - [H2C manual V1.0 section 11.12.2.2 p.170](https://csm.bblcdn.com/hub/eff78da43720461787dc8bbe5fa0372d.pdf), [replace induction hotend](https://wiki.bambulab.com/en/h2c/maintenance/replace-induction-hotend)
- **Auto hotend-switch sequence (toolhead <-> rack).** Unlock the latch -> place the right hotend on the toolhead (if any) into an empty rack slot, preferring the lowest-numbered (closest) empty slot -> install the new hotend -> lock the latch. This describes the generic mechanism, not a mid-job event. - [rack workflow](https://wiki.bambulab.com/en/h2c/manual/induction-hotend-rack-workflow)
- **Filament-to-nozzle mapping.** Bambu Studio matches AMS filament to a hotend by material type + closest colour from the project, and also weighs the filament last used on each induction hotend; an exact material + colour match maps automatically, a material-only match uses the closest colour. The actual mapping is calculated on the printer at send time from the sliced file's filament settings and the machine's current nozzle status; a poor network connection can make this fail, so filament-info sync must be completed before slicing. - [H2C manual V1.0 section 6.1.2 p.78](https://csm.bblcdn.com/hub/eff78da43720461787dc8bbe5fa0372d.pdf)
- **Send page.** Shows per filament: material/colour match result, storage location ("Ext" for external spool, or AMS letter + number), and assigned induction hotend location ("R" = on the right toolhead, otherwise rack position 1-6). Non-matching nozzles (wrong diameter/flow from slicing) are greyed out when remapping manually; remapping that increases filament use shows an estimated increase. Manual remapping needs firmware 01.01.00.00 or later. - [H2C manual V1.0 section 6.1.2 p.78-79](https://csm.bblcdn.com/hub/eff78da43720461787dc8bbe5fa0372d.pdf)
- **Sync info.** "Sync info" pulls nozzle diameter, flow settings and nozzle configuration from both the toolhead and the induction hotend rack before slicing. - [H2C manual V1.0 section 5.4 p.56](https://csm.bblcdn.com/hub/eff78da43720461787dc8bbe5fa0372d.pdf)
- **Mixed diameters/flows (firmware-gated).** Bambu Studio does not support slicing with hotends of different diameters, including left vs right (still true at firmware 01.02.00.00; the H2C FAQ repeats it). Same diameter, different flow is supported: the system sets nozzle flow to "Hybrid", preferring the standard-flow nozzle and assigning the high-flow one only when it adds no extra purge. Firmware 01.01.00.00 added manual filament-to-hotend mapping, mixed-flow same-diameter support, and per-hotend Read. - [H2C manual V1.0 section 5.4 p.56](https://csm.bblcdn.com/hub/eff78da43720461787dc8bbe5fa0372d.pdf), [H2C FAQ](https://bambulab.com/en-us/h2c/faq), [firmware release history](https://wiki.bambulab.com/en/h2c/manual/h2c-firmware-release-history)
- **Send-time safety check.** Every send compares the hotend on the toolhead against the hotend type in the sliced file; the live-view camera also detects the toolhead hotend type to flag a mismatch before it causes a failed print. - [H2C manual V1.0 section 6.5.3 p.100-101](https://csm.bblcdn.com/hub/eff78da43720461787dc8bbe5fa0372d.pdf)
- If the manual and the firmware release history disagree, the firmware release history is the authority (the manual's own stated rule, p.1 "Version and Update"). - [H2C manual V1.0](https://csm.bblcdn.com/hub/eff78da43720461787dc8bbe5fa0372d.pdf), [firmware release history](https://wiki.bambulab.com/en/h2c/manual/h2c-firmware-release-history)

## In practice
- With one AMS on the right inlet, put every AMS filament on the RIGHT extruder (Custom grouping). Each filament can get its own induction hotend: no flush, small prime only.
- Left extruder = external spool or a second AMS on the lower inlet.
- Keep every object inside X 25-325 unless you know which hotend prints it; check object positions per plate before sending.
- Run "Sync info" before slicing, not after: the send-page mapping is calculated on the printer from the sliced file, so a stale sync can make automatic mapping fail or pick the wrong hotend.
- Do not swap a hotend mid-print expecting the job to pick up the change; the only documented "Read after swap" workflow is a maintenance procedure between jobs.
- A greyed-out nozzle when remapping on the send page is a diameter/flow mismatch against the slice, not a bug.

## Not verified / open questions
- Dual-nozzle maximum Z (320 vs 325 mm): wiki and manual logic say 320 mm, store says 325 mm; unresolved. Plan to 320 mm until Bambu reconciles it.
- Whether swapping a hotend mid-print is possible at all, and what happens to the job's filament/nozzle mapping if it is: no official source.
- Exact moment a hotend's memory is written: Bambu's marketing says the hotend can automatically store filament information but never says when.
- The release date of firmware 01.01.00.00: the release history gives no date for that entry.
