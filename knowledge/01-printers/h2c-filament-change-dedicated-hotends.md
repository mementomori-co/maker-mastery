# H2C filament change with dedicated hotends (one induction hotend per filament)
Applies to: H2C · Bambu Studio 02.08.02.61 (source and profiles at tag v02.08.02.61) · firmware: not checked (M620/M628 semantics are printer-side and undocumented)
Verified: 2026-10-05

Manual = H2C 3D Printing User Manual V1.0 2026.01, printed page numbers (PDF page = printed + 1).

## Facts

### Change sequence (physical)
- Swap in three steps: (1) the toolhead moves right, unlocks the current right hotend and places it in an empty dock on the rack; (2) per the slicing settings it fetches the next induction hotend and locks it on; (3) induction heating brings it to temperature and it extrudes a small amount onto the prime tower to balance extrusion pressure. - [manual section 6.1.1 p.77](https://csm.bblcdn.com/hub/eff78da43720461787dc8bbe5fa0372d.pdf)
- Parking dock: the system picks the empty dock with the smallest number (closest to the toolhead); with docks 1, 3, 5 empty, dock 1 is used first. - [rack workflow](https://wiki.bambulab.com/en/h2c/manual/induction-hotend-rack-workflow)
- An empty dock must exist for the swap. - [manual p.77](https://csm.bblcdn.com/hub/eff78da43720461787dc8bbe5fa0372d.pdf)
- A hotend swap still loads and unloads filament through the AMS: the manual says switching induction hotends reduces purging but takes longer overall because of AMS loading and unloading. - [manual section 6.2.1 p.83](https://csm.bblcdn.com/hub/eff78da43720461787dc8bbe5fa0372d.pdf)
- A shorter AMS-to-printer PTFE tube reduces material-change time (included tube 900 mm, minimum 600 mm when the AMS 2 Pro or AMS HT sits on top of the printer). - [manual p.37](https://csm.bblcdn.com/hub/eff78da43720461787dc8bbe5fa0372d.pdf)
- Switching between the left and right hotends is the most efficient change, reducing both purge volume and switching time. - [manual p.83](https://csm.bblcdn.com/hub/eff78da43720461787dc8bbe5fa0372d.pdf)

### G-code (H2C 0.4 mm machine template)
- The `change_filament_gcode` template contains `M620 S[next_filament_id]A H[next_hotend]` (line 13), `T[next_filament_id] H[next_hotend]` (line 93) and `M621 S[next_filament_id]A` (line 179). `H-1` means "fixed hotend per filament, not a dynamic nozzle map", not an error: `next_hotend` is -1 unless a filament uses different hotends on different layers (`GCode.cpp` L117, `MultiNozzleUtils.cpp` L188). Which rack hotend the printer picks for `H-1` is decided printer-side (not verified). - [change_filament_gcode template](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bambulab/BambuStudio/v02.08.02.61/resources/profiles/BBL/machine/Bambu%20Lab%20H2C%200.4%20nozzle%20template%20change_filament_gcode.json), [GCode.cpp](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bambulab/BambuStudio/v02.08.02.61/src/libslic3r/GCode.cpp), [MultiNozzleUtils.cpp](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bambulab/BambuStudio/v02.08.02.61/src/libslic3r/MultiNozzleUtils.cpp)
- Unload side order: `M620.11 O1 T[filament_retract_length_nc]` is template line 57, before the `M628 S1` ... `M629` block (lines 67-77) that holds the per-type `M620.11 S... E-{retraction_distances_when_cut[...]}` lines. - [change_filament_gcode template](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bambulab/BambuStudio/v02.08.02.61/resources/profiles/BBL/machine/Bambu%20Lab%20H2C%200.4%20nozzle%20template%20change_filament_gcode.json)
- `filament_retract_length_nc` (label "length when change hotend": filament retracted inside the hotend before a hotend change): default 10 mm, max 18 mm; `Bambu PLA Basic @BBL H2C` sets 18 mm. - [PrintConfig.cpp L4994-5003](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bambulab/BambuStudio/v02.08.02.61/src/libslic3r/PrintConfig.cpp), [Bambu PLA Basic @BBL H2C.json](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bambulab/BambuStudio/v02.08.02.61/resources/profiles/BBL/filament/Bambu%20PLA%20Basic%20@BBL%20H2C.json)
- Template flush time: `SYNC T{ceil(flush_length / 125) * 5}` (line 116) sits outside the `{if flush_length > 0}` block (opens line 119) and evaluates to 0 s at flush 0; only the heat/cool SYNCs (lines 121-125) are inside the `if`. - [change_filament_gcode template](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bambulab/BambuStudio/v02.08.02.61/resources/profiles/BBL/machine/Bambu%20Lab%20H2C%200.4%20nozzle%20template%20change_filament_gcode.json)

### Flush and tower prime
- Flush is non-zero only when the target hotend is non-empty and holds a different filament (`Print.cpp` L3806; `volume_to_purge` starts at 0 at L3804, and an empty nozzle also gets flush 0). With one hotend per filament, flush = 0. - [Print.cpp](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bambulab/BambuStudio/v02.08.02.61/src/libslic3r/Print.cpp)
- A tower prime is still planned per change (`plan_toolchange(..., wipe_volume_ec, wipe_volume_nc, volume_to_purge)`, Print.cpp L3825-3826). A same-extruder, different-hotend change uses `wipe_volume_nc` (WipeTower.cpp L2924). - [Print.cpp](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bambulab/BambuStudio/v02.08.02.61/src/libslic3r/Print.cpp), [WipeTower.cpp](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bambulab/BambuStudio/v02.08.02.61/src/libslic3r/GCode/WipeTower.cpp)
- `filament_prime_volume_nc` (label "Hotend change": volume to prime the extruder for a hotend change on the tower): default 60 mm3; `Bambu PLA Basic @BBL H2C` = 30 mm3 (`filament_prime_volume` 30 mm3 inherited from `@base`). - [PrintConfig.cpp L2932-2938](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bambulab/BambuStudio/v02.08.02.61/src/libslic3r/PrintConfig.cpp), [Bambu PLA Basic @BBL H2C.json](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bambulab/BambuStudio/v02.08.02.61/resources/profiles/BBL/filament/Bambu%20PLA%20Basic%20@BBL%20H2C.json), [Bambu PLA Basic @base.json](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bambulab/BambuStudio/v02.08.02.61/resources/profiles/BBL/filament/Bambu%20PLA%20Basic%20@base.json)
- Manual vs marketing: the manual says "nearly 'no purging'" and that the concept excludes the initial purge when filament first enters a hotend (p.77, also p.12-13). The Vortek FAQ's "Zero Purge Waste" wording conflicts with "nearly"; the manual plus the tower prime above decide it. - [manual p.77](https://csm.bblcdn.com/hub/eff78da43720461787dc8bbe5fa0372d.pdf), [Vortek FAQ](https://wiki.bambulab.com/en/h2/manual/vortek-faq)
- `filament_change_length_nc` (label "Hotend change", ooze control: extrude a length from the old nozzle before the change): default 10 mm; PLA Basic @BBL H2C = 4 mm. - [PrintConfig.cpp L2834-2840](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bambulab/BambuStudio/v02.08.02.61/src/libslic3r/PrintConfig.cpp), [Bambu PLA Basic @BBL H2C.json](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bambulab/BambuStudio/v02.08.02.61/resources/profiles/BBL/filament/Bambu%20PLA%20Basic%20@BBL%20H2C.json)

### Purge mode (Standard / Prime Saving)
- Labels (from source): Prepare > Filament section title bar > "Purge mode" button > dialog "Purge Mode Settings" > **Standard** (full purging with speed and temperature transition, best quality) or **Prime Saving** (less prime waste and faster; may cause slight colour mixing or small surface defects). Config key `prime_volume_mode` (`Default` = Standard, `Saving`). The H2C dialog has no "Fast" option (`support_fast_purge_mode` is 0 in `fdm_machine_common.json`, not overridden for the H2C). The button shows only for multi-nozzle or fast-purge printers (Tab.cpp L5909). - [PurgeModeDialog.cpp](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bambulab/BambuStudio/v02.08.02.61/src/slic3r/GUI/PurgeModeDialog.cpp), [Plater.cpp L2828, L2852](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bambulab/BambuStudio/v02.08.02.61/src/slic3r/GUI/Plater.cpp), [Tab.cpp L5909](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bambulab/BambuStudio/v02.08.02.61/src/slic3r/GUI/Tab.cpp), [fdm_machine_common.json](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bambulab/BambuStudio/v02.08.02.61/resources/profiles/BBL/machine/fdm_machine_common.json)
- Prime Saving forces both `wipe_volume_ec` and `wipe_volume_nc` to 15 mm3 (Print.cpp L3820-3824); the flush multiplier switch applies only to the Fast mode, so flush is unchanged. The prime-volume fields are greyed in Prime Saving (Tab.cpp L4959-4961). Standard follows the filament prime volume fields. The manual calls it "Purge Saving Mode" (p.56): label mismatch between manual V1.0 and source 2.8.2.61. - [Print.cpp](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bambulab/BambuStudio/v02.08.02.61/src/libslic3r/Print.cpp), [Tab.cpp](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bambulab/BambuStudio/v02.08.02.61/src/slic3r/GUI/Tab.cpp), [manual p.56](https://csm.bblcdn.com/hub/eff78da43720461787dc8bbe5fa0372d.pdf)

### Slicer time model (an estimate, not a physical time)
- H2C 0.4 mm machine profile: `machine_load_filament_time` 15 s, `machine_unload_filament_time` 15 s (the common parent's 29/29 is overridden). The estimator bills unload + load whenever the nozzle or the filament in the nozzle changes (`GCodeProcessor.cpp` L5979-5994). Valid while `default_ams_type` stays unset (default -1; no H2C profile sets it): a per-AMS-type load/unload override exists in this tag and a profile update could change the numbers. - [Bambu Lab H2C 0.4 nozzle.json](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bambulab/BambuStudio/v02.08.02.61/resources/profiles/BBL/machine/Bambu%20Lab%20H2C%200.4%20nozzle.json), [GCodeProcessor.cpp](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bambulab/BambuStudio/v02.08.02.61/src/libslic3r/GCode/GCodeProcessor.cpp), [MultiNozzleUtils.cpp](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bambulab/BambuStudio/v02.08.02.61/src/libslic3r/MultiNozzleUtils.cpp)
- `machine_hotend_change_time` = 0 (label "Hotend change time"); `get_hotend_change_time()` is defined (GCodeProcessor.cpp L6260) but not called in GCodeProcessor.cpp, MultiNozzleUtils.cpp or Print.cpp, so the estimator adds no separate hotend-change time. - [fdm_machine_common.json](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bambulab/BambuStudio/v02.08.02.61/resources/profiles/BBL/machine/fdm_machine_common.json), [GCodeProcessor.cpp](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bambulab/BambuStudio/v02.08.02.61/src/libslic3r/GCode/GCodeProcessor.cpp)
- Left <-> right switch: `machine_switch_extruder_time` 5 s (tooltip "For statistics only"), parent `fdm_bbl_3dp_002_common.json`. - [fdm_bbl_3dp_002_common.json](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bambulab/BambuStudio/v02.08.02.61/resources/profiles/BBL/machine/fdm_bbl_3dp_002_common.json)
- Heating rates in the profile: right 13.3 C/s, left 3.5 C/s; cooling right 3.4 C/s, left 1.6 C/s; `enable_pre_heating` = 1 (runtime effect undocumented). - [Bambu Lab H2C 0.4 nozzle.json](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bambulab/BambuStudio/v02.08.02.61/resources/profiles/BBL/machine/Bambu%20Lab%20H2C%200.4%20nozzle.json)
- What the 15 s + 15 s estimate leaves out: the 4 mm old-nozzle ramming (`filament_change_length_nc`), the 1 s reverse travel (`filament_ramming_travel_time_nc` = 1 s for PLA Basic @BBL H2C; WipeTower.cpp L3446-3530, L1956, L3109), the template's Z lifts and travel moves, and the optional prime-tower-interface purge (`M620.13 ... L{filament_tower_interface_purge_volume}`) when that option is on. These land in the Prime tower and travel time of the slice report, not in the 30 s. - [WipeTower.cpp](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bambulab/BambuStudio/v02.08.02.61/src/libslic3r/GCode/WipeTower.cpp), [change_filament_gcode template](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bambulab/BambuStudio/v02.08.02.61/resources/profiles/BBL/machine/Bambu%20Lab%20H2C%200.4%20nozzle%20template%20change_filament_gcode.json)

### What Bambu publishes about change speed (no seconds-per-change figure exists)
- Store: the induction hotend reaches temperature in 8 seconds, with the stated condition that this is based on Bambu PLA Basic printing temperature. The manual p.13 gives the condition as measured at induction hotend printing temperature. - [H2C store](https://us.store.bambulab.com/products/h2c), [manual p.13](https://csm.bblcdn.com/hub/eff78da43720461787dc8bbe5fa0372d.pdf)
- Blog: "Tool changes take only seconds, with virtually no user involvement." (published 2025-11-14). - [Bambu blog](https://blog.bambulab.com/bambu-lab-h2c-where-multi-material-vortek-system-meets-engineering-precision)
- Store example (a two-part 6-colour model): purge and prime tower waste 532 g (H2C) / 3032 g (H2D) / 3917 g (H2S); print time 58 % / 88 % / 100 %. Footnotes: all figures are Bambu Studio calculations, and the Vortek advantage assumes all 6 induction hotends of the same diameter with matching AMS units. - [H2C store](https://us.store.bambulab.com/products/h2c)

### Hotend counts and limits
- Rack docks are numbered 1-6; the toolhead hotend is "R". Up to 6 induction hotends = 1 on the toolhead + 5 parked, so one dock is always empty; plus the fixed LEFT hotend = 7 hotends max. Up to 7 filaments per print without purge waste needs 6 induction hotends + 1 left hotend of the same diameter and matching AMS units. - [manual p.77, p.79](https://csm.bblcdn.com/hub/eff78da43720461787dc8bbe5fa0372d.pdf), [rack workflow](https://wiki.bambulab.com/en/h2c/manual/induction-hotend-rack-workflow), [Vortek FAQ](https://wiki.bambulab.com/en/h2/manual/vortek-faq)
- 24 filament slots max (4 AMS 2 Pro + 8 AMS HT). With more than 7 filament types the system calculates the best combination; with more filaments than hotends, the two filaments with the lowest purge requirement share one hotend. - [manual p.83-84](https://csm.bblcdn.com/hub/eff78da43720461787dc8bbe5fa0372d.pdf), [H2C store](https://us.store.bambulab.com/products/h2c)
- More than two AMS units need the 4-in-1 PTFE Adapter (FAZ013-N). Recommended layout: 2 AMS on the right hotend, 1 on the left. Upper inlet = right hotend, lower = left (manual printed p.32, section 2.4.1); the AMS 2 Pro goes on the upper inlet to use the Vortek change system. - [manual p.32, p.36](https://csm.bblcdn.com/hub/eff78da43720461787dc8bbe5fa0372d.pdf), [H2C store](https://us.store.bambulab.com/products/h2c)
- Without an AMS, each hotend is assumed to have one external spool. - [manual p.84](https://csm.bblcdn.com/hub/eff78da43720461787dc8bbe5fa0372d.pdf)

### Slicer modelling and mapping
- `extruder_max_nozzle_count` = 1 (left) / 6 (right), `master_extruder_id` 2; right variants "Direct Drive Standard" and "Direct Drive High Flow" (the left list also has "Direct Drive E3D High Flow"). `filament_map_2`, `filament_nozzle_map`, `filament_volume_map` are internal (`comDevelop`); `filament_extruder_variant` has no CLI option. - [Bambu Lab H2C 0.4 nozzle.json](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bambulab/BambuStudio/v02.08.02.61/resources/profiles/BBL/machine/Bambu%20Lab%20H2C%200.4%20nozzle.json), [PrintConfig.cpp L2423-2459, L4982-4986](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bambulab/BambuStudio/v02.08.02.61/src/libslic3r/PrintConfig.cpp)
- "Nozzle Volume Type" options: Standard, High Flow, Hybrid, TPU High Flow, E3D High Flow. "Hybrid" is set automatically when mixing flows: it prefers the standard-flow nozzle and uses the high-flow one only when that adds no purging (manual section 5.4 p.56). Mixed hotend diameters are not supported in slicing (p.56). - [PrintConfig.cpp L4852-4865](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bambulab/BambuStudio/v02.08.02.61/src/libslic3r/PrintConfig.cpp), [manual p.56](https://csm.bblcdn.com/hub/eff78da43720461787dc8bbe5fa0372d.pdf)
- `filament_map_mode` values in this tag: "Auto For Flush", "Auto For Match", "Manual", "Nozzle Manual", "Default". Grouping modes in the UI: Filament-Saving (default), Convenience, Custom (manual p.83; labels not re-checked in the 2.8.2 GUI). - [PrintConfig.cpp L2440-2448](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bambulab/BambuStudio/v02.08.02.61/src/libslic3r/PrintConfig.cpp), [manual p.83](https://csm.bblcdn.com/hub/eff78da43720461787dc8bbe5fa0372d.pdf)
- The filament -> hotend mapping is decided at print start: the printer calculates it from the print parameters and the machine's current nozzle status, also using the filament last used on each hotend. The Send page lets you remap manually; non-matching hotends are greyed. After slicing, filaments are bound to their assigned extruder (no switching to the other side at send time). - [manual p.78, p.79, p.84](https://csm.bblcdn.com/hub/eff78da43720461787dc8bbe5fa0372d.pdf)

## In practice
- Read the slicer's time for a one-hotend-per-filament print as "15 s unload + 15 s load per change" plus tower time, and treat it as an estimate. The physical change time has no official figure: time the changes on your own first multi-filament print.
- Keep Purge mode on Standard unless a part tolerates slight colour mixing or small surface defects; Prime Saving cuts the tower prime from 30 to 15 mm3 (PLA Basic @BBL H2C).
- Never describe the H2C as "no purging at all"; say "no flush between dedicated hotends, tower prime remains".
- Slicer-side grouping and CLI mapping facts are in the 02-bambu-studio category.

## Not verified / open questions
- CONTRADICTED (community, do not use): a reseller blog says there is no purging at all and no purge tower when switching between the six hotends. Wrong: manual p.77 step 3 and Print.cpp L3818-3826 keep a tower prime per change. - [additive-x blog](https://www.additive-x.com/blog/bambu-h2c-vortek-explained) (undated, community)
- Official seconds per induction-hotend change or per right-side filament change: none found in the manual, store, blog, rack-workflow wiki or Vortek FAQ. Measure it on your own printer.
- Firmware semantics of `M620 H`, `M620.11`, `M628`, `M629`: no official documentation found.
- Whether the slicer models the first-load "initial purge" anywhere: Print.cpp L3806 gives flush 0 for an empty nozzle; not traced further.
- Which physical rack hotend the printer uses for `T... H-1`: printer-side, not verified.
- Whether a future profile sets `default_ams_type` (would change the 15 s / 15 s numbers).
