# 44 - Safety

Conservative, sourced safety notes for a home print shop. Every number keeps its condition (voltage, current, temperature, exposure time). US workplace rules (OSHA, NIOSH) are quoted as the best available practice; they do not legally bind a home shop, and nothing here replaces the current SDS for your exact product or your local electrical code.
