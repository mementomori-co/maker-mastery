# Drilling polycarbonate sheet (about 3 mm): drill geometry, speed and feed, coolant, edge distance, hole spacing, annealing

Applies to: polycarbonate (PC) sheet and moulded PC enclosure walls of about 3 mm, drilled by hand or on a drill press
Verified: 2026-10-05

All figures are sheet-maker data for Makrolon (Covestro), LEXAN (Polyvantis), PALSUN (Palram) and a Sheffield Plastics guide. A moulded enclosure's resin grade is usually not stated, so treat these as starting points, not that enclosure's own numbers.

## Facts

### Drill geometry, speed, feed
- Covestro Makrolon sheet: clearance angle 5-15 deg, rake 0-5 deg, point angle 110-130 deg, helix 19-40 deg, cutting speed 15-30 m/min, feed 0.1-0.3 mm/rev; no hole size or thickness condition given. - [Covestro Makrolon manual MF0261, section 4.4](https://www.exolongroup.com/index.php/fuseaction/download/lrn_file/mf0261_e_170828.pdf)
- Spindle speed from surface speed: n = 1000 v / (pi d). Example: a 2.5 mm drill at 15-30 m/min gives about 1,910-3,820 rpm (arithmetic). - derived from [Covestro MF0261](https://www.exolongroup.com/index.php/fuseaction/download/lrn_file/mf0261_e_170828.pdf)
- LEXAN: clearance 15 deg, rake 0-5 deg, included tip 120-160 deg, helix 30 deg. Its smallest table row (3 mm hole) gives 1,750 rpm and 125 mm/min feed (about 0.07 mm/rev). - [Polyvantis LEXAN processing guide](https://ff-cms.com/storage/1063/PV_TM_002_ProcessingGuide_EN_01-25_Lores.pdf)
- Palram PALSUN, 3 mm bore: 1,500-1,800 rpm, feed 0.03-0.07 mm/rev; clearance 10-20 deg, rake 0-10 deg, tip 110-150 deg, helix 30 deg, 15-30 m/min. A flatter 140-150 deg tip is for thin 1-2 mm sheet only. - [Palram PALSUN technical guide](https://polycarbonate.com.au/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/PALSUN-Technical-Guide-2016.pdf)
- The makers disagree on feed: Covestro's 0.1-0.3 mm/rev is 1.4-10 x the small-hole feeds of Palram and LEXAN, and none has a row below 3 mm. - same three sources
- Sheffield: drill speed 350-1,750 rpm (1,750 rpm for a 1/8 in drill), 15 deg clearance, 0-5 deg negative rake. Its printed point-angle range is a typo in the PDF, so take no point angle from it. - [Sheffield polycarbonate fabrication guide](https://www.tapplastics.com/image/pdf/Polycarbonate%20Fabrication%20Guide.pdf)
- Tools: new, sharp HSS twist drills or carbide-tipped drills. No maker gives pilot-drill, step-drill or backing-board advice. - [Palram](https://polycarbonate.com.au/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/PALSUN-Technical-Guide-2016.pdf)
- Clamp the work firmly and support it to reduce vibration. - [Polyvantis](https://ff-cms.com/storage/1063/PV_TM_002_ProcessingGuide_EN_01-25_Lores.pdf), [Palram](https://polycarbonate.com.au/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/PALSUN-Technical-Guide-2016.pdf)
- Keep heat to a minimum: clear chips often, lift the drill, cool with compressed air (LEXAN). A clean, smooth, cool bore avoids internal stress; polishing the hole edge helps (Palram). - [Polyvantis](https://ff-cms.com/storage/1063/PV_TM_002_ProcessingGuide_EN_01-25_Lores.pdf), [Palram](https://polycarbonate.com.au/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/PALSUN-Technical-Guide-2016.pdf)
- Mark on the protective film with a soft pencil or felt-tip, never a scribe: PC is notch sensitive and scratches can start cracks. Holes reduce sheet strength and add internal stress. - [Covestro MF0261](https://www.exolongroup.com/index.php/fuseaction/download/lrn_file/mf0261_e_170828.pdf), [Sheffield](https://www.tapplastics.com/image/pdf/Polycarbonate%20Fabrication%20Guide.pdf)

### Coolant and chemicals
- No cutting oil or oil emulsion on PC sheet: Covestro and Bayer warn of stress cracking from additives, Palram says never use them, Sheffield warns of crazing. - [Covestro MF0261](https://www.exolongroup.com/index.php/fuseaction/download/lrn_file/mf0261_e_170828.pdf), [Bayer Makrolon solid sheet brochure](https://polycarbonate.com.au/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/Makrolon-Solid-Technical-2014.pdf), [Palram](https://polycarbonate.com.au/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/PALSUN-Technical-Guide-2016.pdf), [Sheffield](https://www.tapplastics.com/image/pdf/Polycarbonate%20Fabrication%20Guide.pdf)
- Coolant is normally not needed; if it is, use water or oil-free compressed air (Covestro, Palram, Sheffield) or compressed air only (LEXAN). - same sources
- Acetone and xylene can stress-crack PC even at very low stress. - [Polyvantis](https://ff-cms.com/storage/1063/PV_TM_002_ProcessingGuide_EN_01-25_Lores.pdf)
- An older machine-grade Makrolon plate sheet allows light machine oil for routing; it is plate, not sheet, and is not a source for the no-oil rule either way. - [Makrolon MG sheet](https://www.thyssenkrupp-materials-na.com/_legacy/UCPthyssenkruppBAMXNA/assets.files/tkmna_com/products/collateral/plastics/sheet-plastics/makrolonmg.pdf)

### Edge distance and hole spacing
- Covestro (fastening holes): hole centre to sheet edge at least 2 x hole diameter and at least 6 mm. - [Covestro MF0261](https://www.exolongroup.com/index.php/fuseaction/download/lrn_file/mf0261_e_170828.pdf)
- Palram: at least 2-2.5 x d from the edge, minimum 10 mm (centre or edge not stated). LEXAN: at least 1-1.5 x d. - [Palram](https://polycarbonate.com.au/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/PALSUN-Technical-Guide-2016.pdf), [Polyvantis](https://ff-cms.com/storage/1063/PV_TM_002_ProcessingGuide_EN_01-25_Lores.pdf)
- No PC maker gives a minimum hole-to-hole spacing or web width for perforation patterns (full-text searched in all the guides above). The only spacing numbers are for rivets (LEXAN: 5-10 x rivet diameter). - same sources
- A perforated-metal rule (bar width and hole diameter at least the sheet thickness) exists for metal, not PC; use it only as a rough analogy. - [Syrtech perforated metal definitions](https://www.syrtech.com/perforated-metal-definitions)

### Other cutting methods (makers disagree)
- LEXAN does not recommend shearing, punching, tapping, laser or water jet (stress, micro-cracks, poor finish). Covestro: punching is easy up to 3 mm; laser possible after drying, with brown edges above 2 mm. Palram: these methods are possible. Laser makers note discoloration and thin sheet only. - [Polyvantis](https://ff-cms.com/storage/1063/PV_TM_002_ProcessingGuide_EN_01-25_Lores.pdf), [Covestro MF0261](https://www.exolongroup.com/index.php/fuseaction/download/lrn_file/mf0261_e_170828.pdf), [Palram](https://polycarbonate.com.au/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/PALSUN-Technical-Guide-2016.pdf), [Epilog FAQ](https://www.epiloglaser.com/how-it-works/faq/can-you-cut-engrave-polycarbonate-with-laser-machine/)

### Annealing (conditioning)
- Covestro: 120-125 C, 1 h per 3 mm of thickness, cool slowly, ideally in the oven. Bayer: 120-130 C, 1 h per 3 mm, avoid sharp temperature swings. Palram calls annealing complicated and possibly ineffective. No source says to anneal after drilling. - [Covestro MF0261](https://www.exolongroup.com/index.php/fuseaction/download/lrn_file/mf0261_e_170828.pdf), [Bayer](https://polycarbonate.com.au/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/Makrolon-Solid-Technical-2014.pdf), [Palram](https://polycarbonate.com.au/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/PALSUN-Technical-Guide-2016.pdf)

### Community and vendor-blog notes (labelled)
- Community (single forum posts): a standard HSS bit can grab on exit and crack the edge, a step drill worked well; soapy water and pecking to clear flutes were also suggested. No maker corroborates. - [Practical Machinist thread](https://www.practicalmachinist.com/forum/threads/drilling-polycarbonate-so-that-the-hole-stays-clear.229402/)
- Vendor blog: high rpm, constant feed, ease off at exit, positive rake. The positive rake conflicts with the makers' 0-5 deg. - [AttaBox blog](https://attabox.com/blogs/how-drill-holes-polycarbonate-enclosures)

## In practice
- Worked example (2.5 mm vent holes in a 3 mm PC wall): a sharp HSS or carbide drill with 0-5 deg rake, about 1,750-1,900 rpm, light feed about 0.03-0.07 mm/rev (the 3 mm rows of LEXAN and Palram), wall clamped flat on solid support, marks on the film only, peck and clear chips with air, water or air if any coolant, never oil, acetone or xylene.
- Measure the actual wall with calipers; enclosure drawings often dimension only the lid and floor.
- A dense vent pattern with thin webs between holes is neither approved nor ruled out by any PC source. Prove it with a test: drill a small patch of the full pattern in scrap of the same thickness with the same settings, then look at the webs under light. Pass = no white crazing or cracks, smooth bores. If a web cracks, increase the pitch.

## Not verified / open questions
- Minimum web between drilled holes in PC: no source.
- Whether to anneal after drilling: no source; published schedules are for flat sheet, not assembled enclosures with gaskets and inserts.
- Data for holes below 3 mm: no maker table row.
