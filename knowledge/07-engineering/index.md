# 07 Engineering - index

Mechanical design rules for printed parts: fasteners, joints, snaps, stability and heat. Every note lists public sources; numbers from moulding guides need a printed test coupon before they carry load.

- [heat-set-inserts.md](heat-set-inserts.md) - M2-M5 heat-set insert hole diameter, depth and boss rules (SPIROL, PEM, Tappex) with the FDM caveat and community iron-temperature rules.
- [snap-fits-living-hinges-and-mechanisms.md](snap-fits-living-hinges-and-mechanisms.md) - snap-fit beam equations, allowable strain, root fillet and angle rules, PP living hinges, Bambu PLA/PETG Basic inputs and Bambu Studio fit-compensation tools.
- [keyed-joints-and-connectors.md](keyed-joints-and-connectors.md) - how Bambu Studio's Cut-tool connectors apply tolerance, which shapes lock rotation and at what tolerance they spin free.
- [physical-stability.md](physical-stability.md) - tip angle and push-to-tip formulas, real printed mass from slicer output, lowering the centre of mass, tall-thin parts while printing.
- [enclosure-thermal-budget.md](enclosure-thermal-budget.md) - sealed-box temperature rise, forced-air flow, PSU derating curves and printed-wall limits, with a worked example.
- [drilling-polycarbonate-sheet.md](drilling-polycarbonate-sheet.md) - drilling about 3 mm PC sheet: drill geometry, speed and feed, no-oil rule, edge distance, annealing.
