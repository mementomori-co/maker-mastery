# Pre-print checklist

The `print-qa` agent fills one row per item below, in this order, with the tool output that proves each verdict and one fix per FAIL.
Nothing prints on a FAIL. No AI ever starts, sends, uploads or queues a print: you review this checklist and press Print in Bambu Studio yourself.
An item that cannot apply to your printer (for example grouping on a single-nozzle printer) is written "not applicable: <reason>", never left blank.

- [ ] Physical load recorded: `knowledge\00-gear-inventory\current-load.md` exists, carries a `Verified: YYYY-MM-DD` line, and lists every AMS slot or external spool (slot -> brand / material / colour), the nozzle(s) fitted and the plate. Missing or undated = FAIL, and no filament item can PASS.
- [ ] Project filaments = physical filaments, slot by slot (`tools\audit_3mf.py`; no PLA sliced as PETG, no profile for a filament that is not loaded).
- [ ] Grouping (dual-nozzle printers): every plate's filament-to-extruder map (`filament_map_mode`, `filament_maps`) sends each filament to the extruder that can physically reach it (for example AMS filaments on the extruder the AMS feeds, an external spool on the other). Read from `tools\audit_3mf.py`; single-nozzle printers write "not applicable".
- [ ] One material per object unless multi-material is the point of the part (stated in the part spec). PLA and PETG in one object is a FAIL in every case: the two bond weakly.
- [ ] Every object sits inside the printable area of the extruder(s) it uses, per the note for your printer model under `knowledge\01-printers\` (dual-nozzle printers have a smaller area that both nozzles can reach).
- [ ] Prime tower (if any): the tower and its brim, measured from the G-code, clear every object's outline by at least 8 mm (`tools\tower_bounds.py`, `tools\tower_clearance.py --min-gap 8`). The rule and its reason are in `knowledge\02-bambu-studio\grouping-and-purge.md`.
- [ ] Sliced with the installed Bambu Studio CLI (`tools\slice_cli.py`): result Success, `result.json` `warning_message` read and empty or explained, support time equal to what the part spec planned, `enable_arc_fitting = 0` in the G-code header for gated files on printers whose GUI forces it off, and the process preset is the printer's `0.20mm Standard` profile or every deviation is justified on the print sheet with a source.
- [ ] Faces on the plate match the finish the part spec wants (a textured plate gives a matte grain; a smooth plate may need glue for materials other than PLA - check the note for your plate under `knowledge\02-bambu-studio\` or `knowledge\03-materials\`).
- [ ] Time and grams per plate (from `result.json`) are on the print sheet and reviewed by you.
- [ ] Display pieces (anything that stands on its own): `tools\stability_check.py` run with the slicer grams (`--gcode` for parts with supports); centre of mass inside the support polygon (FAIL otherwise); minimum tip angle and first-layer contact reported. Its thresholds (tip under 15 degrees WARN, contact under 25 % of the hull WARN) are proposed, not verified: tilt-test the printed piece and record the result. Functional parts and coupons write "not applicable".
- [ ] Display pieces, visible faces: every visible overhang or flat ceiling is first designed out (an angled underside at 45 degrees or the angle your overhang coupon proves, a split at a natural part boundary with keyed joints, or a new orientation). Support is the last resort, in the same filament (Support > Filament for support > Support/raft interface = Default), with a written reason why support scars beat sag on that face. Every Support > Advanced > Top Z distance value is labelled "not verified" until a support-removal coupon is scored (`tools\support_gap_readback.py` reads the printed gap back from the G-code). A large flat visible top states its Ironing Type (Quality > Ironing) and the sheet states and justifies all four Strength-tab patterns (Top surface, Bottom surface, Internal solid infill, Sparse infill) per `knowledge\02-bambu-studio\pattern-selection-guide.md`. Support coverage of visible overhangs is checked with `tools\overhang_gate.py`.
- [ ] Hash line: the verdict header states the full sha256 of the gated `_SLICED.3mf` and of each of its `plate_N.gcode` files, each on the same line as the full path, hashed on the files as judged. A file whose hash changes after the gate is no longer gated.
- [ ] Re-slice rule: the print sheet tells you to print the gated `_SLICED.3mf` directly, without re-slicing; if Bambu Studio forces a re-slice, compare the new time and grams with the gated numbers on the sheet before printing, and stop on a mismatch. The sheet carries those gated numbers.
- [ ] Purpose and visible-part ratio: the print sheet states the print's PURPOSE in one line (display, stream, functional, coupon). For a display or stream print, run `tools\visible_ratio.py <plate_N.gcode> --out-prefix work\visible_ratio\<stem>-plate<N>` on every plate with support, and report the final ratio for the front and iso cameras, the ratio at 25 / 50 / 75 % height, the face-region ratio when a `--face-mask` file exists, and look at both PNGs (grey = visible part, BLUE = part hidden behind support, RED = support). Exit 2 (final ratio below 0.60) = FAIL whatever the support coverage says; exit 1 (below 0.80) needs a written reason on the sheet. The thresholds are proposed, not verified. A functional or coupon print writes "not applicable: <purpose>".
- [ ] Tree support trunk size: for any plate with tree support, report every trunk's diameter at the plate and at z 50 mm (from the G-code support extrusion, per connected trunk) and the Branch diameter and Branch diameter angle in use, with what the angle adds per 100 mm of height (about 2 x tan(angle) x 100 mm). A long trunk wider than 12 mm at the plate needs a written reason; a too-slender trunk gets a local brace, never a whole-tree fattening. A plate without tree support writes "not applicable".
